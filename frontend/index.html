Scene conditions

Line: raw confidence. Dashed: calibrated confidence. Camera and image modes: the whole frame is scanned at several sizes by a classifier trained on 483 drone photos, so a drone can be found even when it is not moving (for example a picture held up to the camera). Each candidate must stay in approximately the same place for 3 scans in a row. Up to 6 candidates can be displayed. A face detector (OpenCV Haar cascade) only blocks windows that sit on the face. Lower Strictness finds more drones but gives more false alarms; if something is wrongly flagged, hold it in view and press “Not a drone (3 s)” so the detector adapts to your camera. Weights come from train_drone.py.
